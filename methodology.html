How Stedrok Ranks Stocks

Transparent, repeatable, data-driven scoring

The Four Pillars

Stedrok evaluates every stock across 4 dimensions:

📊 Value (40%)

Measures: How cheap is the stock relative to earnings and assets?

  • P/E Ratio: Lower = cheap relative to profits
  • P/B Ratio: Lower = cheap relative to book value
  • P/CF Ratio: Lower = cheap relative to cash flow

🏆 Quality (30%)

Measures: How strong is the business fundamentally?

  • ROE: Higher = better profits on shareholder capital
  • Debt/Equity: Lower = less financial risk
  • Revenue Growth: Consistent 5%+ = healthy organic growth

🛡️ Safety (Gate)

Measures: Can this company survive a downturn?

  • Current Ratio: Assets ÷ Liabilities > 1.5
  • Debt Service: Can pay interest 3x over
  • No Recent Losses: 2+ years of profitability

If SAFETY fails, stock auto-fails screening. Quality and Value don't matter if company might go bankrupt.

⚡ Dip Timing (30%)

Measures: Has the stock recently fallen relative to its history?

  • 52-Week Change: Down 20%+ from high = high dip score
  • Momentum: Recent downward trend
  • Technical Bounce: Oversold indicators (RSI < 30)

Total Score = The Final Grade

Score = (Value × 0.40) + (Quality × 0.30) + (Dip × 0.30)

  • 40% Value: The stock must be cheap — that's the foundation
  • 30% Quality: But it must be a good business, not a value trap
  • 30% Dip Timing: And ideally bought after a recent decline

⚠️ Safety is a GATING REQUIREMENT: If a stock fails safety checks, it's immediately disqualified.

AVOID <50 Overvalued, low quality, or unsafe. Doesn't fit our criteria.
