How Stedrok Ranks Stocks

A transparent, repeatable, data-driven approach for value-oriented investors

Our Philosophy

Stedrok is built for conservative, long-term investors who want to identify companies trading below their intrinsic value. We focus on businesses with strong fundamentals, resilient balance sheets, and current prices that offer a margin of safety.

Every stock in our universe is evaluated using four pillars, which together form a comprehensive view of each investment opportunity.

The Four Pillars

Every company is scored using four pillars: Value, Business Strength, Resilience, and Price Context. Each pillar blends multiple metrics – no single ratio makes or breaks the score.

Value – what we pay

We look at whether the share price is modest relative to the company's profits, assets, and cash generation. The goal is to favour businesses that are sensibly priced rather than speculative.

Examples of what feeds into this pillar include earnings, book value, and cash flow relative to price.

Business Strength – how the company performs

This pillar focuses on the underlying business: returns on capital, use of debt, and the consistency of growth. Strong, disciplined companies with healthy economics are scored more favourably.

We place more weight on persistent profitability and sensible leverage than on short‑term headlines.

Resilience – ability to withstand stress

Before anything else, we ask a simple question: can this company reasonably survive a downturn? We look at liquidity, balance sheet strength, and a track record of staying out of persistent losses. If a company fails these resilience checks, it does not move forward in the process.

Price Context – where we are in the range

Even a strong business at a fair valuation can become more attractive after a pullback. This pillar considers how the current share price compares with its recent history and trend, with an emphasis on orderly declines rather than extreme volatility.

Total Score – a single view of the opportunity

Behind the scenes, each pillar is normalised and combined into a single score. Valuation carries the most influence, followed by business strength and price context. Resilience acts as a strict safety gate – if a company does not pass our resilience checks, it will not receive a high score regardless of how cheap it looks.

The exact weightings and thresholds are kept flexible so we can refine the model over time as new data and research become available.

How to read the Stedrok rating

Rating What it means
BUY Represents our highest conviction ideas based on the model. These names combine attractive valuation, strong underlying business quality, and a solid resilience profile.
WATCH Interesting candidates that pass our core checks but may need either a better entry price or further improvement in fundamentals.
AVOID Companies that currently sit outside our preferred range on valuation, quality, or resilience. These typically do not merit further work for conservative, value‑oriented investors.

Update Frequency

Daily: All rankings are recalculated every trading day using the latest market data.

Data Source: Yahoo Finance API, SEC filings, real-time price data.
