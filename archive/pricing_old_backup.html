Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free tier work?

Create an account and get access to 3 randomly selected stocks updated daily. No credit card required. Upgrade anytime for full access to 100+ stocks.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel online at any time from your account or PayPal. No lock-in contracts.

What payment methods do you accept?

All PayPal payment methods: credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal balance. Secure and encrypted.

What if my payment fails?

We'll retry automatically for 3 days. You'll get an email if we need you to update your payment method.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. You'll receive a reset link via email.

Is this financial advice?

No. Stedrok provides an educational research tool and model-based stock scores. It does not provide personal financial advice or recommendations.
