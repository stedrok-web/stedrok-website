About the Platform

What is Stedrok?

Stedrok is an independent global value stock screener built for long-term investors. Every trading day, the engine scans 5,900+ listed companies across 40+ global exchanges and ranks them on a four-pillar framework: intrinsic value discount, business quality, balance sheet resilience, and price context.

The output is a focused ranked list of the strongest fundamental candidates — updated twice daily, with a research note on every name (Pro).

Is this financial advice?

No. Stedrok provides general information for research and screening purposes only. Nothing on this site constitutes personal financial advice. Scores are model outputs based on public financial data. Always conduct your own due diligence before making any investment decisions.

What exchanges and countries are covered?

Stedrok covers 5,900+ companies across 40+ global exchanges, including the ASX (Australia), NYSE and NASDAQ (USA), LSE (UK), TSX (Canada), and major European and Asian markets. All companies are scored on the same framework regardless of geography, enabling apples-to-apples comparison across markets.

How is the scoring done?

Each company is scored across four pillars: (1) Value — is it cheap relative to intrinsic worth? (2) Quality — is the business fundamentally strong? (3) Resilience — can it survive a downturn? (4) Price Context — is it at an interesting entry point? The combined score produces the ranked list. Full details are on the Methodology page.

How often is the data updated?

The screener runs twice per trading day. Scores and rankings are refreshed with each run. The timestamp of the most recent batch is shown on the Dashboard. Stedrok is designed for patient, fundamental-focused investors — not for intraday trading.

Data and Privacy

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. All payments are processed entirely by PayPal. Stedrok never handles or stores your card details. Your billing relationship is directly with PayPal.

What data do you collect about me?

Stedrok collects only the information needed to operate your account: your email address and subscription status. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Where is the data sourced from?

All fundamental data (revenue, earnings, balance sheet, valuation ratios) is sourced from public company financial disclosures and aggregated market data providers. Anomaly detection scripts run on each batch to flag inconsistent records before scores are published.
