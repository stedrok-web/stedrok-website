Data Sources and Quality

Company financial data (earnings, cash flow, balance sheet items) and price data are sourced from third-party financial data services, including Yahoo Finance data services. These are aggregated third-party feeds — they are not primary exchange data and carry the inherent limitations of their underlying sources, including potential delays, data gaps, corporate-action adjustments, and provider errors. Stedrok does not itself originate or warehouse primary financial statement data.

Data is refreshed on each scoring run (typically 3x daily). All input fields are passed through an automated anomaly-detection step before scoring begins. Records with missing, stale, or inconsistent data are flagged and excluded or downweighted before publication.

Despite these controls, data errors do occur. Financial API data for smaller or less-covered companies can be incomplete or delayed. Stedrok scores should be treated as a starting point for research, not as a definitive statement of a company's financial position.

Model Methodology Summary

The scoring engine applies a transparent, rules-based framework across four pillars: value, quality, resilience, and price context. The full methodology is published at Methodology. Weights, thresholds, and scoring logic are documented and applied identically across all covered securities.

The AI Hybrid layer takes shortlisted Core candidates through an additional validation step: cross-referencing current financial context using AI language model analysis before publication. AI Hybrid results are published when internal data-quality and confidence checks indicate sufficient support. LLM outputs are probabilistic and may contain errors or inconsistencies; they are not reviewed by a licensed financial professional prior to publication.

Model Limitations

Past performance is not indicative of future results. Any historical scores, rankings, or backtest results published by Stedrok reflect past market conditions only and do not guarantee any future outcome. Actual investor results may differ materially.

The Core scoring model is backward-looking. It uses reported financial data only — it does not incorporate analyst forecasts, management quality assessments, qualitative competitive analysis, or macroeconomic outlooks. A high Stedrok score does not predict that a share price will rise. A low score does not predict that it will fall. Markets are uncertain and losses are always possible.

The AI Hybrid layer uses large language models (including Google Gemini). LLM outputs are probabilistic and can produce inaccurate, incomplete, or internally inconsistent results — including hallucinations or misinterpretations of financial data. AI-assisted research outputs have not been audited by a licensed financial professional. Users should treat AI-enhanced views with additional caution and verify key data points independently.

The model applies the same ruleset across all geographies and sectors. Sector-specific accounting norms, regulatory differences, and local market conditions are not individually calibrated. Users researching companies in unfamiliar markets should apply additional judgment.
