Research Trust and Disclosures

This page explains how Stedrok operates, where data comes from, and what this model does and does not do.

Stedrok currently publishes two research views: Core (rules based) and AI Hybrid (AI-assisted with separate guardrails). Blended is a comparison view that combines both in one ranked table.

Entity

Stedrok Pty Ltd is based in Australia and operates as an independent research platform.

Scope

Core and AI Hybrid outputs are for idea generation and research workflow support, not personal financial advice.

Data and Refresh

Public market and fundamentals data are processed on a recurring trading-day schedule.

Conflicts

No paid stock placement is used in ranking output. Educational pages are process-focused.

What Stedrok Does Not Do

  • No personal advice or suitability assessment.
  • No performance guarantees or return promises.
  • No automated execution on user behalf.
  • No claim that one score replaces due diligence.
Research only, not financial advice. Always evaluate decisions against your own objectives, risk tolerance, and jurisdictional requirements.
