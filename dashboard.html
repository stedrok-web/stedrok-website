Understanding the Metrics

Value: Quantifies undervaluation relative to intrinsic worth. Higher scores reflect deeper discounts to fair value.

Quality: Measures financial health, competitive positioning, and operational efficiency.

Risk: Evaluates balance sheet strength and earnings stability. Higher scores indicate greater resilience in downturns.

Dip: Captures recent price weakness from historical peaks and highlights pullbacks that may be interesting for further research.

Confidence: Indicates the strength of the data behind the score on a 0 to 100 scale. Higher values reflect more complete and consistent financial data.

Discount: Current price versus estimated fair value. Positive percentages indicate undervaluation.

Research Status: BUY highlights the strongest current combination of valuation, business quality, resilience, and price context. WATCH warrants further review. AVOID sits outside the preferred range under current conditions.
