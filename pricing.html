Why Upgrade to Pro

How Pro users typically use the platform to enhance their research workflow.

Monitor High-Conviction Ideas

Access Core, AI Hybrid (beta), and Swarm-Lite views in one account. Core applies the standard rules-based framework. AI Hybrid adds a separate validation step - each shortlisted result is cross-checked against live data and current context using an AI layer before publication. Swarm-Lite runs 300 deterministic agents across BASE, BULL, BEAR and CRISIS scenarios. Each agent scores independently; picks that survive all four scenarios earn the highest Swarm Score and represent the strongest cross-scenario consensus.

Run a Disciplined Dollar-Cost Averaging Plan

Use the ranked list to pick one or two names each week, then place small recurring orders through your chosen broker. Stedrok is research only and does not execute trades or hold client money. The engine refreshes twice each trading day so your next decision is always based on current data.

Export for Deeper Analysis

Download the full ranked list as CSV and integrate with your own spreadsheets, backtesting tools, or portfolio models. Combine Stedrok scores with your proprietary research.

Track Changes Over Time

See how company ratings evolve as new data arrives. Identify which businesses are improving fundamentals versus those deteriorating, helping you stay ahead of the market.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about billing, features, and account management.

How Does the Free Tier Work?

Create an account and get instant access to 3 BUY-rated stocks from the current daily list. There's no credit card required and no expiry date. We intend to keep a free tier available long-term, and if that policy changes we will provide notice.

Can I Cancel My Pro Subscription Anytime?

Yes. You can cancel online at any time from your Account Settings page or directly through PayPal. There are no lock-in contracts, no cancellation fees, and your access continues until the end of your current billing period.

What Payment Methods Do You Accept?

All PayPal payment methods are supported: credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal balance. Payments are securely processed by PayPal using industry-standard encryption. Stedrok never sees or stores your card details.

When Will I Be Charged?

Your first charge occurs immediately when you subscribe. Subsequent charges occur on the same day each month (e.g., if you subscribe on the 15th, you'll be charged on the 15th of every month). You'll receive an email receipt for each payment.

What If My Payment Fails?

If a payment fails, we'll automatically retry for 3 days. You'll receive an email notification asking you to update your payment method. Your account will remain active during this grace period. If payment isn't resolved after 3 days, your account reverts to the free tier.

What Happens If I Cancel?

Your Pro access continues until the end of your current billing period. After that, your account automatically reverts to the free tier. You keep your account, saved preferences, and historical data - you just lose access to the full ranked daily stock list and premium features.

Can I Get a Refund?

Yes. If you're not satisfied within 30 days of your first payment, email us at contact@stedrok.org for a full refund. We typically process refunds within 3 business days. After the 30-day window, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis (e.g., technical issues, billing errors).

Do You Offer Annual Billing or Discounts?

Currently we only offer monthly billing at $5/month. We keep pricing simple and transparent. There are no annual plans, no enterprise tiers, and no promotional discounts. Everyone pays the same fair price.

What Is AI Hybrid?

AI Hybrid is a separate research process available to Pro members alongside the standard Core view. It takes shortlisted companies through an AI-assisted validation step, cross-checking data against live external sources and current context before results are published. It is designed to catch discrepancies and stale data that a purely rules-based model may not detect.

AI Hybrid runs independently from Core and does not alter Core ratings. It is currently in beta, and not all Core results will carry a corresponding AI Hybrid result at all times.

Is This Financial Advice?

No. Stedrok is a stock research platform. The scores and ratings are model outputs based on historical data and public information. They are designed for screening and research, not for automatic buy or sell decisions. Always consider your own objectives, financial situation, and risk tolerance.

What If I Have Billing Issues or Questions?

Contact us at contact@stedrok.org with "Billing" in the subject line. Pro subscribers receive priority support with responses typically within 24 hours. Free tier users receive responses within 3 business days.
