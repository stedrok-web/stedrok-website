Frequently asked questions

Common questions from new users.

Is this financial advice?

No. Stedrok is an educational research tool. The scores are model outputs based on historical data and public information. They are designed for screening and research, not for automatic buy or sell decisions. Always consider your own objectives, financial situation, and risk tolerance before making investment decisions.

What stocks are covered?

Stedrok currently covers 100+ companies across global markets, with a focus on established businesses that have sufficient trading history and financial reporting. Coverage is expanding over time as data quality and model confidence improve.

How often is data updated?

Market prices and scores are refreshed once per trading day. Financial statement data is updated shortly after companies report earnings. We prioritise accuracy over real-time speed – this is a tool for long-term investors, not day traders.

Can I really use the free tier forever?

Yes. The free tier gives you permanent access to 3 ranked stocks. It's not a trial – there's no time limit. If those 3 stocks are enough for your needs, you never have to pay. Upgrade to $3/month when you want access to the full list of 100+ companies.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel any time from your Account Settings page or directly through PayPal. There are no lock-in contracts or cancellation fees. If you cancel within 30 days of your first payment, you're eligible for a full refund.

Is my payment information secure?

All payments are processed by PayPal. Stedrok never sees or stores your credit card details. Your billing information stays with PayPal, a PCI-compliant payment processor.

Can I export the data?

Yes. Paid subscribers can export the full ranked stock list to CSV format. This makes it easy to integrate Stedrok data into your own spreadsheets or research workflow.

What if I disagree with a rating?

That's completely fine. Stedrok is a starting point, not an oracle. If your own research leads you to a different conclusion, trust your judgment. The model can't incorporate factors like management quality, competitive moats, or your personal risk tolerance.
