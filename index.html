The Fundamental Metrics Retail Investors Ignore
And Institutions Use

Professional analysts at hedge funds and asset managers run systematic fundamental screens every single day. They look for ROIC trends, free cash flow yield, balance sheet stress signals, and price-to-intrinsic-value gaps. Retail investors rarely have access to the same framework at scale. That's the gap Stedrok was built to close.

Chasing Narratives, Not Numbers

Most retail platforms rank stocks by momentum, analyst ratings, or social sentiment. Institutions measure ROIC trends, FCF yield, and margin of safety. These are fundamentally different signals — and conflating them is how retail investors consistently overpay.

Black Boxes Dressed as Insights

A tool that gives you a score with no auditable methodology is marketing, not research. If you can't trace a rating back to specific financial metrics — P/FCF, net debt ratio, earnings stability — you have no basis for trusting it when the market turns.

What the Model Actually Measures

Four pillars. Specific metrics. Auditable logic. No guesswork.

Pillar 1 — Value
Is it cheap relative to intrinsic worth?
DCF discount to fair value · Price-to-FCF · EV/EBITDA · Price-to-Book
Pillar 2 — Quality
Is the business actually good?
Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) · Gross & operating margins · Earnings stability · Revenue consistency
Pillar 3 — Resilience
Can it survive a downturn?
Net Debt / EBITDA · Interest coverage · Current ratio · Balance sheet stress score
Pillar 4 — Price Context
Is it at an interesting entry point?
% below 52-week high · Distance from recent support · Short-term price momentum context

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from new users.

Is This Financial Advice?

No. Stedrok is a stock research platform. The scores are model outputs based on historical data and public information. They are designed for screening and research, not for automatic buy or sell decisions. Always consider your own objectives, financial situation, and risk tolerance before making investment decisions.

What Stocks Are Covered?

Stedrok scans thousands of companies across global markets and publishes a ranked daily list that typically includes 50+ BUY candidates. Coverage keeps expanding as data quality and model confidence improve.

What Is AI Hybrid?

AI Hybrid is a separate research layer available to Pro members. It takes companies shortlisted by the Core model through an additional AI-assisted validation step - cross-checking data against live sources and current context before results are published. It runs independently from Core and does not change Core ratings.

AI Hybrid is currently in beta. Results are published when the validation step meets the integrity threshold, so not every Core result will always have a corresponding AI Hybrid result.

How Often Is Data Updated?

Market prices and scores are refreshed 3x daily. Financial statement data is updated shortly after companies report earnings. This is built for long-term investors, not day traders.

Can I Use the Free Tier Long-Term?

Yes. The free tier gives you ongoing access to 3 BUY-rated stocks from the current daily list. There is no credit card requirement and no expiry date. We intend to keep a free tier available long-term, and if that policy changes we will provide notice. Upgrade to $5/month when you want the full ranked daily list, typically including 50+ BUY candidates from our global scan.

How Do I Cancel My Subscription?

You can cancel any time from your Account Settings page or directly through PayPal. There are no lock-in contracts or cancellation fees. If you cancel within 30 days of your first payment, you're eligible for a full refund.

Is My Payment Information Secure?

All payments are processed by PayPal. Stedrok never sees or stores your credit card details. Your billing information stays with PayPal, a PCI-compliant payment processor.

Can I Export the Data?

Yes. Paid subscribers can export the full ranked stock list to CSV format. This makes it easy to integrate Stedrok data into your own spreadsheets or research workflow.

What If I Disagree with a Rating?

That's completely fine. Stedrok is a starting point, not an oracle. If your own research leads you to a different conclusion, trust your judgment. The model can't incorporate factors like management quality, competitive moats, or your personal risk tolerance.
