Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from new users.

Is This Financial Advice?

No. Stedrok is a stock research platform. The scores are model outputs based on historical data and public information. They are designed for screening and research, not for automatic buy or sell decisions. Always consider your own objectives, financial situation, and risk tolerance before making investment decisions.

What Stocks Are Covered?

Stedrok scans thousands of companies across global markets and publishes a ranked daily list that typically includes 50+ BUY candidates. Coverage keeps expanding as data quality and model confidence improve.

How Often Is Data Updated?

Market prices and scores are refreshed at least once per trading day, and in practice the engine usually runs twice each trading day. Financial statement data is updated shortly after companies report earnings. This is built for long term investors, not day traders.

Can I Use the Free Tier Long-Term?

Yes. The free tier gives you ongoing access to 3 ranked preview stocks from the current data batch. There is no credit card requirement and no expiry date. We intend to keep a free tier available long-term, and if that policy changes we will provide notice. Upgrade to $3/month when you want the full ranked daily list, typically including 50+ BUY candidates from our global scan.

How Do I Cancel My Subscription?

You can cancel any time from your Account Settings page or directly through PayPal. There are no lock-in contracts or cancellation fees. If you cancel within 30 days of your first payment, you're eligible for a full refund.

Is My Payment Information Secure?

All payments are processed by PayPal. Stedrok never sees or stores your credit card details. Your billing information stays with PayPal, a PCI-compliant payment processor.

Can I Export the Data?

Yes. Paid subscribers can export the full ranked stock list to CSV format. This makes it easy to integrate Stedrok data into your own spreadsheets or research workflow.

What If I Disagree with a Rating?

That's completely fine. Stedrok is a starting point, not an oracle. If your own research leads you to a different conclusion, trust your judgment. The model can't incorporate factors like management quality, competitive moats, or your personal risk tolerance.
