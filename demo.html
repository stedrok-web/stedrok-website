Pillar Scoring Snapshot

The model scores each company across four pillars, then combines them into a final ranked output.

Value

Measures valuation gap versus estimated fair value. Higher values indicate deeper discount and stronger margin-of-safety context.

Quality

Tracks business durability and operating quality, including profitability consistency and balance-sheet discipline.

Risk

Prioritizes downside resilience. Strong scores indicate more robust financial structure under tougher macro conditions.

Dip

Adds price-context awareness by quantifying pullback structure relative to historical range and valuation support.
