Learn Value Investing Fundamentals

By Stedrok Research Team

This learning page is built for long-term investors who want a repeatable framework. It explains value investing, intrinsic value, and margin of safety in plain language.

Value Investing Basics

Compare price with business value and avoid weak balance sheets. Focus on durability before upside narratives.

Intrinsic Value Basics

Intrinsic value is an estimate from future cash flows and resilience assumptions. Use ranges and scenarios, not one fixed number.

Margin of Safety Basics

A valuation buffer helps absorb model error and volatility. Cheap multiples alone are not enough without quality support.

How Stedrok Applies This

Stedrok combines value, quality, resilience, and price context to rank candidates for research workflow support.

Quick FAQ

What does value investing focus on?

Durable fundamentals, sensible pricing, and downside risk control over the long run.

Is intrinsic value exact?

No. It is an estimate that should be treated as a range and refreshed when fundamentals change.

Why margin of safety matters?

It provides a cushion when assumptions are imperfect or macro conditions become unfavorable.
