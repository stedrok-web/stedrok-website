Core Concepts to Anchor Every Decision

Value Investing Basics

Compare market price with business value and avoid weak balance sheets. Durability and downside protection come before upside narratives.

Intrinsic Value Basics

Intrinsic value is a scenario-based estimate from cash flow strength and operating resilience. Treat it as a range, not a single precise number.

Margin of Safety Basics

A valuation buffer helps absorb model error, macro shocks, and execution drift. Cheap multiples alone are not enough without quality support.

Cash Flow and Valuation

How to Read Free Cash Flow Yield Without Overpaying for Growth

Separate high-quality cash generation from one-off spikes. Pair free cash flow yield with reinvestment quality, capital intensity, and valuation discipline before sizing.

Glossary terms: Free Cash Flow, Free Cash Flow Yield, Margin of Safety. Apply in Methodology and Demo.

Trap Detection

How to Avoid Value Traps When a Stock Looks Cheap

Distinguish temporary sentiment pressure from structural deterioration. Focus on balance sheet stress, cash conversion, and weakening business quality behind low multiples.

Glossary terms: Price to Earnings Ratio, Drawdown, Debt to Equity Ratio. Apply in Methodology and Demo.

Balance Sheet Risk

How to Judge Balance Sheet Risk Before You Average Down

Evaluate leverage, liquidity coverage, and refinancing pressure before adding to a declining position. Cheap entry points do not offset fragile capital structures.

Glossary terms: Debt to Equity Ratio, Current Ratio, Margin of Safety. Apply in Methodology and Demo.

Valuation Ranges

How to Interpret Discount to Estimated Value as a Range

Treat discount signals as a probability range across base, bull, and downside cases. Decision quality improves when valuation is tied to evidence strength and resilience risk.

Glossary terms: Margin of Safety, Free Cash Flow Yield. Apply in Methodology and Demo.

Drawdown Analysis

How to Separate Temporary Drawdowns from Structural Decline

Use drawdown context with cash flow and profitability quality to avoid catching falling knives. A lower price helps only if business durability remains intact.

Glossary terms: Drawdown, Free Cash Flow, Return on Equity. Apply in Methodology and Demo.

Quality Confirmation

How to Use ROE with Cash Flow Quality to Confirm Strength

High ROE can be strong or misleading depending on leverage and cash conversion. Confirm quality by testing whether reported profitability translates into repeatable free cash flow.

How to Evaluate Management Capital Allocation Discipline

Assess whether cash is allocated to high-return reinvestment, prudent debt reduction, and shareholder-aware policy. Strong allocation compounds intrinsic value over time.

Quick FAQ

What does value investing focus on?

Durable fundamentals, sensible pricing, and downside risk control over the long run.

Is intrinsic value exact?

No. It is an estimate that should be treated as a range and refreshed when fundamentals change.

Why margin of safety matters?

It provides a cushion when assumptions are imperfect or macro conditions become unfavorable.
